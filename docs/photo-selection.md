# Homepage photo selection

The club-life preview uses the school's own ball-control training frame from
https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbirWUnqSjC/.
The local source is `club-life/DbirWUnqSjC-01.webp` in the retained club archive.
It is copied byte-for-byte as `assets/club-ball-training.webp` (960 × 540).

The previous 480 × 640 warm-up image was enlarged and cropped into a landscape
box. The selected frame has twice the source width and shows children, balls,
training equipment and the supervising coach in one scene. It is displayed at
its original 16:9 ratio on desktop and mobile, without cutting off people.

The homepage retains one archive preview and the two original coach portraits.
The sand-training image is already 1600 × 900 and stays in its video tile. No
duplicate gallery, generated replacement or artificial sharpening is added.
This is an editorial image choice; no conversion uplift is claimed.
